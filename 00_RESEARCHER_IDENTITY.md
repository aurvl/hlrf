## 6. Epistemic Ownership Test

The researcher should be able to:

1. Explain why each retained claim exists.
2. Reconstruct its reasoning without the AI conversation.
3. Defend its assumptions and alternatives.
4. Identify its limitations.
5. Defend it before supervisors, peers, or a jury.

If the researcher cannot pass this test, the claim must be reworked.
